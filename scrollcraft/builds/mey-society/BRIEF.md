# Mey Society: brief

Brief built from a short interview (four multiple-choice questions) plus the copy and brand of the existing site in `mey-society/`. The answers are in the user's words; items marked **[authored]** are my decisions, made where the interview did not reach.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe:** "Del día a la noche". **[authored]** Three words: Mediterranean light, discreet, nocturnal. References: the light of an Ibiza day from noon to midnight; the brand's chrome monogram.
2. **Journey:** **[authored]** from the existing site's order. One message → what we solve → sea and home → everything on offer → the night → how it works → your message.
3. **Energy:** **[authored]** calm at noon, rising through the afternoon, a held breath at nightfall, the most intense moment at night, then quiet at the close.
4. **What people should feel, and the moment they remember:** "La isla se enciende".
5. **What no other site does:** **[authored]** scrolling turns the day into night, and the island lights up point by point. Each light is a service you can tap, and your WhatsApp message writes itself from what you lit.
6. **Range:** "Premium minimal".
7. **One world or separate scenes:** "Escenas separadas". The scenes are separate, but one sky runs underneath them all. It is the clock, not a camera flight.
8. **Existing assets:** the logo (`logo.png`) and the copy. No photos. kie.ai is not reachable from this session, so the visuals are SVG and CSS, not generated photos. This is the main limitation of the build.

## Who it is for and what it must leave behind

- **What it is:** a private concierge in Ibiza. Boats, villas, cars, VIP tables and private chefs, all through a single conversation.
- **What the visitor should believe by the end:** one message and the whole island is taken care of.
- **Single action:** "Escríbenos", the same label everywhere. The site has no WhatsApp number configured yet, so for now it opens an email with the message pre-filled.

## Grammar

**[authored] New grammar: "Day clock".** Each scene is an hour of the day. The nav is a clock that advances with the scroll, and its hours are clickable stops. The sky is one continuous layer: no hard cuts of the ground and no section counters. The ending is midnight with the island lit, and the CTA carries the message the visitor built. Bans: `scrub`, `spotlight`, `count` (there are no real figures), hard cuts of the ground, section counters.

Why the defined grammars lost:
- **Filmic one-shot:** the user asked for separate scenes.
- **Continuous world:** same reason, and it needs video.
- **Chaptered editorial:** it bans the gradual drift, and the brief is a gradual sunset.
- **Live surface:** this is not a software product.
- **Typographic poster:** a luxury brand needs place, not just type.
- **Gallery:** its labels are anti-persuasion, and a concierge has to persuade.
- **Split stage:** there is no two-sided argument.
- **Cutlist:** premium minimal is the opposite of a cut every second.

**The peak, in a visitor's words:** "it got dark and the whole island started lighting up, and I tapped the places I wanted".

**It's the site where** night falls as you scroll, Ibiza lights up place by place, and tapping the lights writes your message to the concierge.

**Signature move:** the island's lights, driven by scroll, double as buttons for building the plan. The plan travels to the close, and the message is composed from it live.
